How to read the field

  • Jagged outline: rock. It falls straight.
  • Striped bars with inward marks: neon gate. Fly the gap.
  • Diamond with side wings and a square eye: drone. It weaves left and right.
  • A hit ends the run. Score grows while you last, plus a bonus for each dodge.
